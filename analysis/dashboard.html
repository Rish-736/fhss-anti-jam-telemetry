Anti-Jam Telemetry Link

frequency-hopping ground station · 433 MHz connecting…

A drone telemetry link that hops between five radio channels. Before each hop the transmitter listens to the channel it is about to use, and skips any channel that sounds busy. Click a channel below to block it and watch the link route around it.
